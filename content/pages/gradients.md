---
title: Gradient viewer
description: Plot 400 × 3 matrices of gradient values as an interactive 3D scatter, one timepoint or many.
---

Paste 400 points of three gradients and explore them in 3D. Gradient 1 runs along x, gradient 2 along y and gradient 3 along z. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two-finger drag to pan.

Load a folder of files (one per timepoint, named in order) to watch the gradients change over time with the slider.
